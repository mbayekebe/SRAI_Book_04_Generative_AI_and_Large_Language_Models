# Generative AI and LLM Foundations

PU-B04-C01 introduces the technical foundation for SRAI Book 4 and the evidence discipline that governs its complete learning pathway.

The production unit combines a 42-page controlled lesson, a portable executable notebook, separate assessment materials, an executive brief, a narrated presentation and a public video lesson.

## Central idea

A language model generates statistically plausible continuations. Factual authority must come from evidence, validation and accountable human use.

## Book 4 promise

Ambitious learners will progress across Book 4 toward building, training, evaluating and explaining a small educational GPT-style model.
